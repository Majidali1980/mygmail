# AI Lead Magnet

Here’s a complete Lovable AI prompt for your AI-powered lead generation email web app:

Build a modern AI-powered SaaS web application for lead generation and automated outreach emails.

App Name:
"AI Outreach Studio"

Purpose:
This web app helps digital marketing agencies automatically generate professional outreach emails by uploading a business poster, flyer, service image, or business details.

Core Features Required:

1. Gmail Integration

* Connect with real Gmail accounts using OAuth authentication.
* Users can send and receive emails directly inside the app.
* Sync inbox, sent emails, drafts, and replies.
* Real-time email notifications.
* Allow AI-generated emails to be edited before sending.

2. AI Poster & Image Analysis

* User uploads:

  * Cleaning services posters
  * Plumbing service posters
  * Movers posters
  * Restaurant ads
  * Real estate flyers
  * Any business marketing material
* AI extracts:

  * Business name
  * Phone number
  * Email
  * Services offered
  * Branding colors
  * Social media links
  * Website details
* OCR text extraction from images and PDFs.

3. AI Email Generator
   After extracting details, AI automatically generates:

* Cold outreach emails
* Lead generation emails
* Digital marketing proposals
* Social media marketing offers
* Website redesign offers
* Branding upgrade offers

The generated email should professionally offer:

* Social media post designing
* 30-day content strategy
* Facebook & Instagram management
* Story creation
* Facebook cover redesign
* Logo redesign
* Analytics & engagement improvement
* Lead generation campaigns
* Branding consultation

4. AI Generated Email Tone
